    CfE planning links and verification¶

    Checked on 27 October 2026 against Education Scotland’s official documents:

    1. Technologies Experiences and Outcomes, Computing Science table, printed page 8.
    2. Technologies Benchmarks, March 2017; Computing Science Third Level printed pages 18–19 and Fourth Level printed pages 23–24; general assessment guidance at the front.
    3. Education Scotland Es&Os landing page and Benchmarks landing page provide the official document context and current download routes if a direct URL changes.

    The links below are planning interpretations, with short paraphrases rather than a reproduction of national standards. Check the complete official text when planning or moderating. Matching a code in a lesson does not demonstrate achievement of the entire outcome.

    Code Outcome focus (paraphrased) Planned opportunity and evidence Limit of the claim
    TCH 3-13a Explain information processes and their use in solving problems Lessons 3–6 and 12: explain filtering, counting and sorting as parts of an analysis pipeline Pupils must describe the process; calling a function alone is insufficient
    TCH 3-13b Use an information model to describe aspects of a system Lessons 1, 7, 14, 17: explain record/field choices, identifiers and omitted information Synthetic data models an imagined system; discuss transfer to real systems and the model’s limits
    TCH 3-14a Language constructs for structured information Lessons 1–8: explain DataFrame/Series access and Boolean masks; trace code This covers a selected structured-information context, not all computing technology learning
    TCH 4-13a Compare real-world solution processes and justify a choice Lessons 8, 13, 16–18: compare cleaning or analytical alternatives and defend a method Fourth Level extension evidence requires explanation and comparison, not just completing the core exercise
    TCH 4-13b Informal comparison of algorithm correctness and efficiency Lesson 6 stretch: compare a maximum scan with a simple paper sorting process, test ties and count comparisons Optional focused opportunity only; use of Pandas sorting is not itself algorithm analysis
    TCH 4-14a Textual language constructs and data structures Lessons 4, 8, 12, 17: explain and adapt Python expressions, a loop and tabular structures Broad programming coverage is not established by this unit alone

    The Third Level benchmark context includes identifiers, sorting/searching, structured information and explaining code. This pack develops a subset of those ideas. It does not teach database design, web markup, architecture or networking. Additional numeracy and literacy opportunities include averages, percentages, chart interpretation and evidence-based writing; no unverified outcome codes are assigned to those links.

    Use several forms of evidence and professional dialogue, rather than a separate tick-box judgement for each benchmark. Consider independence, breadth, challenge and transfer in the wider programme. S3 year group does not prescribe an individual learner’s level. No direct equivalence is asserted here between CfE Fourth Level and SCQF Level 4, or between this pack and any National Qualification. Preparation for later study is a teaching intention, not certification or verified coverage of a qualification specification.
